10 Best Chrome Extensions for Writers in 2026

By Michael Lip May 28, 2026 Guide · 14 min read

Chrome extensions have become essential tools in a writer's workflow. The right combination of grammar checkers, style analyzers, and productivity tools can eliminate friction between thinking and publishing. But with hundreds of writing extensions available, separating signal from noise takes effort.

We tested and ranked 10 Chrome extensions across three categories: grammar and correction, style and readability, and productivity. Each was evaluated on accuracy, speed, pricing, and how well it integrates into real writing workflows—not just demo scenarios. Use the filters below to narrow the list to what matters for your work.

How We Chose These Extensions

Every extension on this list was tested across the same five writing scenarios: a 500-word blog draft, a formal email to a client, a creative fiction passage, a technical document with code samples, and social media copy. We evaluated:

  • Accuracy: False positives, missed errors, and quality of suggestions
  • Speed: Time from typing to seeing suggestions or results
  • Integration: How well it works in Google Docs, Gmail, WordPress, and other common platforms
  • Privacy: Whether text is sent to servers, and what data is retained
  • Value: Feature-to-price ratio at each pricing tier

Ratings reflect real-world Chrome Web Store ratings as of May 2026. Pricing is for individual plans billed monthly.

Quick Recommendations

Best overall: Grammarly remains the most polished grammar extension with the widest platform support. Best for non-native speakers: BeLikeNative is the only tool with L1-aware corrections for 80+ native languages. Best free: LanguageTool offers real multilingual grammar checking with a generous free tier. Best for style: Hemingway is unmatched for readability and conciseness analysis.
