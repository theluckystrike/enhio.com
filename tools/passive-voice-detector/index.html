📖 When passive voice is acceptable

  • Scientific writing — focus on process, not agent ("The solution was heated to 80°C")
  • Unknown agent — "The window was broken" (who broke it?)
  • Object emphasis — "The Mona Lisa was painted by da Vinci" (focus on painting)
